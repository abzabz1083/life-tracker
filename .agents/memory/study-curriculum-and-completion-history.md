name: Study curriculum and completion history
description: The Life Tracker user expects their syllabus in Study and completion timestamps to support activity-pattern tracking.

Populate the user's supplied study curriculum in the Study area with its scheduled weekdays; do not leave it only in a review or attachment. Record task and module completion timestamps and retain completion events so the user can see when they are most active.

**Why:** The user repeated that the curriculum was missing and specifically asked to see when they complete tasks most often.

**How to apply:** When revising Study or task tracking, preserve curriculum schedule visibility and make completion-time history durable, including after an item is reopened.
