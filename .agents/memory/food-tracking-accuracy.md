---
name: Food tracking accuracy
description: The user's accuracy requirement for nutrition logging and estimates.
---

Food tracking should prioritize accuracy. The user asked for “0 errors ever”; do not promise that nutrition data can be error-free. Prefer package-label values for branded foods, clearly identify AI estimates, and require explicit confirmation before logging an estimate. Do not silently substitute ambiguous cuts or preparation methods.

**Why:** The user explicitly emphasized that food tracking must be accurate.

**How to apply:** For food search, package photos, AI estimates, and meal logging, favor label-backed data and make source and uncertainty clear.
